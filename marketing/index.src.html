Questions

Which browsers does it work with? %icon:caretDown%

Dia, Arc, Chrome, Brave and Edge, or a built-in Chromium with its own profile. Pick one in the panel; Gofer reopens it with remote control turned on and your tabs restored. Chrome and Edge don't allow that for your main profile, so they get a separate one.

Do I need an API key? What does it cost? %icon:caretDown%

Yes, at least one. Groq and Google Gemini both have free tiers that cover everyday use, and Z.ai, DeepSeek and OpenRouter work too. The panel shows today's estimated spend, and you can set a daily limit.

Direct mode uses SubQ Snap, which costs about $0.05 per million input tokens.

Can it do things I didn't ask for? %icon:caretDown%

Anything that saves, pays, sends or deletes waits for Allow, and Esc stops a task at any time. Treat that as a safeguard rather than a guarantee: don't connect a browser profile with accounts you wouldn't want an automated click near.

What happens to the tab I'm using? %icon:caretDown%

The panel's header always shows which tab Gofer is working in. When a task needs another site, it opens a background tab instead of taking yours, and Go back to where I was puts your tabs back afterwards.

It got stuck on a login or a captcha. %icon:caretDown%

Press Take over. The task pauses after its current step, you do what's needed in the browser, then Hand back and it looks at the page again and carries on. It asks for this itself when a page needs you.

Does it work on Windows or Linux? %icon:caretDown%

There are builds on the release page, but it's built and tested on macOS. Keychain passwords, Liquid Glass and switching browsers are Mac-only for now.

macOS says it can't check the app for malware. %icon:caretDown%

The app isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet. Right-click it in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. You only need to do this once.
