Works with

Your browser, with your tabs and sign-ins

It quits and reopens the browser with remote control turned on. Your tabs come back.

The AI you have a key for

Free tiers are enough for everyday use. Add more than one and it switches when one is busy.

It keeps up with the conversation.

Every message is done step by step in your real browser, by an agent that remembers what you've said and what it did.

It runs on your Mac.

There's no Voice Browser account and no server of ours in the middle. You bring the keys; the app talks to your browser and to the AI providers you chose.

  • Local serviceThe app's service listens on 127.0.0.1 only, so nothing else on your network can reach it.
  • Your keysAPI keys live in a file on your Mac and are read only by that service. The panel never sees them.
  • PasswordsStored in the macOS Keychain. Models, history and logs only ever see the placeholder.
  • What models seeThe page's text, its buttons and links, and a screenshot when it helps. Password fields are never read.
  • Activity logsKept on your Mac for 7 days so you can see what the agent did. Turn them off with one setting.

Questions

Which browsers does it work with? %icon:caretDown%

Dia, Arc, Chrome, Brave and Edge, or a built-in Chromium with its own profile. Pick one in the panel; Voice Browser reopens it with remote control turned on and your tabs restored. Chrome and Edge don't allow that for your main profile, so they get a separate one.

Do I need an API key? What does it cost? %icon:caretDown%

Yes, at least one. Groq and Google Gemini both have free tiers that cover everyday use, and Z.ai, DeepSeek and OpenRouter work too. The footer of the panel shows today's estimated spend, and you can set a daily limit.

Direct mode uses SubQ Snap, which costs about $0.05 per million input tokens.

Can it do things I didn't ask for? %icon:caretDown%

Anything that saves, pays, sends or deletes waits for Allow, and you can stop a task with Esc at any time. Treat that as a safeguard rather than a guarantee: don't connect a browser profile with accounts you wouldn't want an automated click near.

What happens to the tab I'm using? %icon:caretDown%

The panel's header always shows which tab the agent is working in. When a task needs to go to another site, it opens a background tab instead of taking yours, and Go back to where I was puts your tabs back afterwards.

It got stuck on a login or a captcha. %icon:caretDown%

Press Take over. The task pauses after its current step, you do what's needed in the browser, then Hand back and it looks at the page again and carries on. It asks for this itself when a page needs you.

Does it work on Windows or Linux? %icon:caretDown%

There are builds on the release page, but it's built and tested on macOS. Keychain passwords, Liquid Glass and switching browsers are Mac-only for now.

macOS says it can't check the app for malware. %icon:caretDown%

The app isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet. Right-click it in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. You only need to do this once.
